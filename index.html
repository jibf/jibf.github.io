Binfei Ji （姬彬斐）

M.S. in Computer Science · Georgia Tech

I am a master's student in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, specializing in Machine Learning and expecting to graduate in December 2026. I am advised by Prof. Yingyan (Celine) Lin in the EIC Lab and am currently a Research Intern at Adobe Firefly, working on audio-visual understanding.

Before returning to graduate school, I spent four years at XPENG working on autonomous driving perception and prediction. I received my B.S. in Physics from Peking University's Yuanpei College in 2021, where I conducted undergraduate research on time projection chamber detectors in Qite Li's group.

My interests include multimodal learning, video understanding, and post-training. I am particularly interested in how reinforcement learning can help models understand and reason about complex, dynamic environments.
